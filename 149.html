Finally a proper watch again

The G-Shock watch I bought in the Black Friday sale arrived quickly, and so far, I love it.

I’ve had several G-Shocks through the years. After being stuck with Fitbits and Apple Watch for however many years, it feels great to finally wear a proper watch again. No daily charging, no distractions and no connectivity issues (or, indeed, connectivity!).

The vibrating alarm, which was the hard requirement for it to replace my Apple Watch, seems to do its job well. The real test, though, will be to see whether it wakes me up tomorrow! 😰
