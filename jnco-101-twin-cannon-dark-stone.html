JNCO 101 Twin Cannon in dark stone

This was my first pair of JNCOs and the start of my collection. I bought them after seeing some guy wearing them and thinking, hey, I want those!

They’re probably one of the most mainstream JNCO models available, and they’re really easy to wear. With 26” leg openings, they’re basically just baggy jeans at (or just ever so slightly beyond) the top of the scale of the mainstream trend for baggy jeans. They feel great and look great, though, even if (or maybe because) they aren’t that extreme.

The embroidery on the back pocket is perhaps a little bit meh compared to other models, but that also makes them relatively discreet.

The flasher is rather basic, which I suppose is to be expected for proper volume products like these.
