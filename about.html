About

Hi, I’m Magnus.

I’m originally from Sweden, but I moved to the UK 19 years ago.

I work at a tech startup in Shoreditch and live in a flat with my husband.

Things I like

I like to optimise and curate things, whether it’s stationery, my personal finances, my possessions, computer setup or life in general. Often, I spend a lot of time overthinking things, but that’s just part of the fun!

I enjoy doing little personal digital projects and creating things digitally, like this blog.

What I miss in London, though, is the sea. I love boating. I grew up by the sea and still have a little rowing boat back in Sweden.

Aside from that, I also like simple food, having a beer, travelling a couple of times a year and enjoying art, design and music.

I also like to read, but how much I actually do it varies a lot. Sometimes, I read several books back to back, but often nothing for months, and never anything longer than 280 pages. I strongly believe that if you can’t tell your story using fewer pages than that, you’re just not a good enough storyteller!

All in all, I’m quite happy to be me and don’t take myself too seriously.

If you want to know a bit more about me, check out my Now page. I’ve also written a post about my values.

Why the blog?

This blog is an expression of my longing for the Internet as it used to be, before algorithms and the hunt for clicks and likes took over. Back then, people just posted whatever they liked, without worrying too much.

It’s mainly a personal journal and a collection of photos, mixed with some random thoughts. I post for myself, and the value I get comes purely from creating something on my own.

If anyone comes across my posts, that’s nice, but if they don’t, that’s fine too.

If I’m honest, the near-zero readership is actually rather liberating. I just post whatever I feel like, without any need to come across as interesting, successful, competent or professional.
