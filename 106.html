Saturday photo dump

A nice autumn day in photos:

  1. Tried Beringer’s, City Island for brunch. Nice food and very convenient, since it’s close to home.
  2. We’re hopefully getting close now to exchanging the contract on the new flat now, so we went looking for a new kitchen.
  3. We were planning to go to Epping Forest, but there was no Central Line, so we ended up in Greenwich instead
  4. I learned that the Prime Meridian by the observatory hasn’t been the actual prime meridian since 1974. The real one is 102 metres from there!
  5. From Greenwich, we walked south across the heath to get to Blackheath, where we took the bus back home.
