Got some new JNCOs from the Black Friday sale

I’ve just added the fifth pair of JNCOs to my collection: a pair of jet-black Low Down Decks.

Compared to my other JNCO jeans, these were a rather impulsive purchase. Previously, I always found the Low Downs a bit weird, but seeing a guy wearing them IRL in Tate Britain last weekend changed my mind. They’re actually a nice, chill pair of jeans.

So when the Black Friday sale came up, I decided to give them a go, and I absolutely love them!

As much as I love and enjoy my other pairs, I would not wear the 50” Convicts to the office, but these I think I will.
