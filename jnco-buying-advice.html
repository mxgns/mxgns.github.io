JNCO buying advice

If you’re thinking about buying your first pair of JNCOs, I’d say go for it! You won’t regret it.

Which model to start with?

As tempting as it is to buy the widest JNCOs possible, the 50” Convicts may not be the best place to start if you’re new to JNCO and want jeans you will actually wear.

Instead, Twin Cannon is probably the most mainstream option, and a good place to start. They are wide enough to be wider than what most people mean by “baggy jeans”, but they are still easy to wear and not as crazy as some other models.

However, if I started from scratch now, I would probably pick the Jesters. They are the same fit as the Twin Cannon, but with nicer details.

What’s the waist size like?

Don’t get fooled by the wide legs. JNCO’s waist sizes are just like any other jeans. I’m normally a 32”, and it’s the same for JNCO. That means the wider models have leg openings wider than the waist.

What’s the length like?

I recommend sticking with your normal jeans length, not longer. I normally wear a 30” inseam for jeans, and that’s the length I wear for JNCO too. Even so, particularly for the wider models, the JNCOs drag on the ground a bit, and I step on the hems.

What’s the quality like?

Sadly, I never wore JNCOs in the 90s, so I can’t compare, but I’d say the modern ones are very good quality. I love how heavy the material is, and they are definitely well-made.
