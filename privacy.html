Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

You can contact me at hello@mxgns.uk.

This website involves personal information in two ways: website analytics (which pages are visited) and the guest book (messages you choose to leave, protected from spam by Cloudflare Turnstile). Each is explained separately below, followed by sections that apply to both.

1. Website analytics

I collect basic analytics to see which pages are visited and how many times. When you load a page, a small script notes the page, the time and, if you followed a link here from another website, that website’s domain. I never record the full URL, path or query parameters of that link — only the domain.

To avoid counting the same visitor twice in one day, the server creates a daily identifier by combining the IP address with today’s date and a so-called salt, after which it discards the IP. The identifier changes every day, and individual records, including any referring domain, are deleted after 3 days. Only the total page visits and referrer totals are kept.

I don’t use any of this to identify you, build a profile, track you across sites, target ads, or make decisions about you.

Purpose and lawful basis

I use this to produce daily statistics on page visits and referring websites, and to improve the site.

My lawful basis is legitimate interests: understanding how the site is used while collecting as little information as reasonably possible.

Retention

Individual analytics records, daily identifiers and any referring domain are deleted after 3 days.

The resulting aggregate page-visit and referrer statistics do not identify individual visitors and are retained indefinitely.

Opting out

You can object to this analytics processing at any time.

After you opt out, the website stops sending analytics events from your browser while the opt-out cookie remains present, and any analytics already recorded for you that day are deleted immediately.

Current analytics status:

Analytics are enabled. You may opt out.

Analytics are disabled. You may opt back in.

2. Guest book

The guest book lets you leave a public message. It is entirely optional.

What I collect

  • Name — whatever you choose to enter. It doesn’t need to be your real name.
  • Website — optional. If you enter one, it is shown as a clickable link next to your entry.
  • Message — the text you write.
  • Date and time — when you submitted the entry, shown next to it.

I don’t store your IP address or browser details with your entry, I don’t collect your email address, and I don’t use the guest book to identify you, profile you or contact you.

Your entry is public

Your name and message are published as soon as you submit them. If you provide a website URL, it is held back until I have reviewed it for spam and security purposes. I may approve it for publication or remove it.

Once published, anyone can read your name, message and, if approved, your website URL. Your entry’s submission date is also shown. Published information may be indexed by search engines or copied or archived by others, which I can’t control.

Please don’t include anything you wouldn’t want to be public, and don’t include other people’s personal information.

Spam prevention (Cloudflare Turnstile)

To keep out bots, the guest book form uses Cloudflare Turnstile. It loads only on the guest book page, not elsewhere on the site.

When the form loads, your browser connects to Cloudflare, so Cloudflare receives signals such as your IP address, TLS fingerprint and browser (user-agent) details, which it uses to tell humans from bots. Turnstile then gives your browser a token, which my server sends to Cloudflare to check. I only receive the pass/fail result, not the signals Cloudflare assessed.

Cloudflare processes these signals on my behalf to protect the form. It also acts as an independent controller of the signals to improve Turnstile’s bot detection, under its own terms; see the Cloudflare privacy policy and its Turnstile privacy addendum. You can’t opt out of Turnstile and still submit an entry. The only way to avoid it is not to use the guest book.

Purpose and lawful basis

  • Publishing your entry: to run a guest book where visitors can leave messages. I rely on your consent, which you give by submitting the form after being told that your name and message will be published immediately. If you provide a website URL, you consent to its publication if I approve it after review. You can withdraw your consent at any time by asking me to remove your entry (see below).
  • Spam prevention and security: to stop automated abuse of the form. I rely on legitimate interests: keeping the guest book usable without asking you for an account or personal details.

Retention and removal

Published entries are kept until you ask me to remove them or I delete them.

Website URLs are kept pending review. I may approve them for publication or reject and delete them if they appear to be spam or pose a security risk.

Entries or URLs I reject when reviewing them are deleted immediately.

To have an entry removed or corrected, email me at hello@mxgns.uk with the name and message (and website, if any) so I can find it. I’ll act on this promptly. I can’t remove copies made by others, such as search engines or web archives.

3. Cookies and browser storage

Analytics does not use cookies, local storage or session storage. If you opt out, this website sets a cookie named _notrack solely to remember your choice and prevent future analytics events from being sent. The cookie expires after 2 years.

This cookie is not used for analytics, advertising or tracking. If you delete the cookie, use another browser or use another device, you need to opt out again.

Guest book: the guest book itself sets no cookies. However, Cloudflare Turnstile may use cookies or browser storage (such as local storage) on the guest book page to work out whether you are human; Cloudflare’s cookie policy has details. This is used only for spam prevention and security and is strictly necessary for that purpose, which is why I don’t ask for consent for it. It is not used for advertising.

4. Sharing and service providers

I use Cloudflare to host this website and to store guest book entries (in Cloudflare D1) on my behalf. For hosting and storage, Cloudflare may process the information only to provide the service to me and may not use it for its own advertising or profiling purposes.

Cloudflare also provides Turnstile, described above. For that service, Cloudflare processes the spam-check data on my behalf to protect the form, and separately as its own controller to improve Turnstile’s bot detection, as described above.

Guest book entries are published on the website and are therefore visible to everyone. I don’t sell or share analytics or guest book information with anyone else or use it for any other purpose, except where necessary for Cloudflare to provide these services.

Cloudflare operates globally, so information may be processed outside the UK. Cloudflare relies on recognised safeguards for these transfers; see its privacy policy for details.

5. Your rights

You may have rights over your personal information, including to object to processing, request access, deletion or correction, restrict processing, and withdraw consent where I rely on it.

Guest book: your entry is identifiable, so I can find it and act on a request. Email me with the details of your entry.

Analytics: because the daily identifier isn’t linked to your name or contact details and is deleted after aggregation, I may not be able to identify which record is yours.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office, though I’d appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

6. Changes to this notice

I may update this notice if the analytics system, the guest book or the way the website processes information changes. The latest version will always be published on this page.
