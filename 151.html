Autumn recap

As we’re quickly approaching the end of November, I figured it’s time for a quick recap of what I’ve been up to this autumn 🍂

  1. Of course, the most significant thing is that my husband and I bought a new flat. I really love how bright it is! Our kitchen renovation has started, with the removal of the old kitchen, and we should hopefully be moving in towards the end of December.
  2. A couple of weeks ago, I went to the Web Summit in Lisbon for work, which made me realise how much the AI experience will go downhill from here.
  3. The only other trip was a day trip to Rye, which was a very nice day out.
  4. My JNCO collection kept growing, adding no less than three new pairs of jeans. A crazy pair of Convicts, a more wearable pair of Jesters and a very sensible pair of Low Downs.
  5. In terms of blog features, I launched a guestbook (feel free to sign it!) and a minor feature for links to related posts. The biggest improvement for me was an update to my blog generator so that I can now post from my iPad, which will be handy for the three trips I’ve got coming up. Lastly, I also added a little feature to jump to a random post.
  6. I bought a camera, a Panasonic Lumix L10. I’m quite excited, but there’s still a lot to learn, so I still seem to default to my phone. My upcoming trip to Japan should be a good opportunity to get more practice.
  7. Culture-wise, there wasn’t a lot happening, but we did go to see My Neighbour Totoro, the musical.

All in all, a pretty quiet autumn. The start of winter is looking to be rather intense, though, with the move and no fewer than three countries to visit 🛫
