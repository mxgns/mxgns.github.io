iPhone Duo-n’t

I went to see the new iPhone Duo today. I have been rather sceptical about the whole thing, but I assumed that once I held it in my hand, it would create the same kind of craving every other iPhone has.

When I first picked it up, it felt as if it might. I really liked the screen’s dimensions when closed. It felt new and fresh, different from the form factor Apple have played on repeat for so long. However, when trying to use it, I realised it’s way too wide to use comfortably with one hand while standing on the tube. And then, for the main trick, when opening the phone up to reveal the folded screen, the whole thing just felt like a slightly awkward iPad mini.

So no, iPhone Duo is not for me. I don’t get what it’s for. And at that price?

Hello, Panasonic Lumix L10

I finally decided to get a real camera again after years of relying on my phone.

I went for the Panasonic Lumix L10, and it seems promising so far, but there are definitely a lot of buttons and options to learn. I seem to have forgotten pretty much everything about how to use a camera!

I don’t necessarily think I will take better photos with this camera than my phone. I know I’m the limitation, not the camera. What I’m hoping for, though, is photos that look a little bit more like photos, without the phone magically compensating for everything. I also think it will make me a slightly more intentional photographer.

And, tbh, I like the idea of being the type of person who carries an actual camera around! 😅
