20 September 2026Day-to-Day

Lunch at Poon’s at Somerset House

Yesterday, we ended up leaving home too late to be in the mood for our usual Saturday brunch routine. Instead, we decided to do lunch at Poon’s, a Hong Kong restaurant in Somerset House.

We chose the 3-course set menu, which changes daily, and were served chicken soup, char siu pork, mushroom salad, and vegetables. It was all very nice, and the quality felt very high. It was also nice to sit at the counter and watch the chefs at work.

We will definitely come back for more.

Note posted 16 September 2026

I installed iOS 27 yesterday. Is it just me, or is it the least exciting iOS release ever? 😔

  • You can make UI elements less transparent after they made them transparent last year.
  • You can make the clock on the lock screen smaller after they made it bigger last year.
  • Genmoji is a bit more reliable, but buggier in other ways.
  • You can AI-manipulate photos in ways that make them no longer representative of the photo you took.

Plus, Siri is allegedly less rubbish, but it’s been bad for so long it’s hard to care.

That’s it? 🤨

End of rant.
