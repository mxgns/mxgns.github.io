End-of-summer recap

Here’s what’s been going on since my “summer so far” recap at the end of July:

  • On an overall level, life has felt a bit in limbo while we’re waiting to complete the purchase and move into our new flat. Living in a temporary place makes everything feel like one big procrastination! 😤
  • I kept spending time playing around with my blog and vibe-coded several new features: a photo gallery, dark mode, comments on posts (but you’ll have to email me your comments, and I post them 😅), site search, a publishing calendar on the archive page and a custom 404 page.
  • I did do a trip, to Sweden, working remotely for a week and taking another week off. I enjoyed nature and my little boat, before it was time to go back.
  • Speaking of travel, I spent hours creating a map of the places I’ve been to.
  • It was good fun going to see the recording of Live at the Apollo.
  • I switched to using Kagi as my search engine, paying a subscription fee to get real search results instead of ads.
  • The best meal I had was at Poon’s in Somerset House.
  • I did a bit of shopping, getting myself an iPad again, a new bag for my EDC and some dungarees. I also bought not one but two pairs of JNCO jeans, which I see as the start of a little collection, with pairs three and four already planned.
  • I rebooted my wardrobe, with the goal of moving from my almost all-black outfits to something more relaxed. It was a fairly thorough process, creating a plan and then a final list, before shopping to plug the gaps and finally doing the switch-over.
  • I also posted about my minimal-ish iPhone setup, my wish list, my little projects, and how I budget.

And now I’m really excited that autumn has arrived. It’s definitely my favourite season! 🍂

20 September 2026Day-to-Day

Lunch at Poon’s at Somerset House

Yesterday, we ended up leaving home too late to be in the mood for our usual Saturday brunch routine. Instead, we decided to do lunch at Poon’s, a Hong Kong restaurant in Somerset House.

We chose the 3-course set menu, which changes daily, and were served chicken soup, char siu pork, mushroom salad, and vegetables. It was all very nice, and the quality felt very high. It was also nice to sit at the counter and watch the chefs at work.

We will definitely come back for more.
