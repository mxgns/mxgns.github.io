Hello, Panasonic Lumix L10

I finally decided to get a real camera again after years of relying on my phone.

I went for the Panasonic Lumix L10, and it seems promising so far, but there are definitely a lot of buttons and options to learn. I seem to have forgotten pretty much everything about how to use a camera!

I don’t necessarily think I will take better photos with this camera than my phone. I know I’m the limitation, not the camera. What I’m hoping for, though, is photos that look a little bit more like photos, without the phone magically compensating for everything. I also think it will make me a slightly more intentional photographer.

And, tbh, I like the idea of being the type of person who carries an actual camera around! 😅

Photo posted 8 October 2026

It’s been a while since I went to the theatre, so I was rather excited to go and see My Neighbour Totoro the musical last night.

And what a treat it was! I sat absolutely fascinated, my mouth open, through most of it. The set design was incredibly clever, whether it was buildings being put together before our eyes or just some cornfields passing by.

I loved how the stage workers, if it’s fair to call them that, were sometimes part of the play, sometimes puppeteers and sometimes just doing what stage workers do.

And the puppets, of course! From the huge Totoro to a flock of chickens. I didn’t think they would do the cat bus, as it seemed too hard to me, but they did!

Definitely go and see it before it’s gone!
