And my fourth pair of JNCOs!

The JNCO Convicts are supercool but admittedly not very versatile. Therefore, I actually bought another pair of JNCO jeans in the same order. I wanted something easy to wear and a break from the dark stone blue of all my other JNCOs, so I went for the Jester in vintage black.

And I absolutely love them! At a modest 26”, they are really easy to wear, and they look great.

After this, I think I need to slow down a bit with the purchases, though! 💸
