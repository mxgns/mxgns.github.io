Wearing JNCOs in 2026

Honestly, “ridiculous” is probably a perfectly fair description of JNCO jeans. They are definitely not everyone’s first choice. And I will feel a bit awkward if I bump into a colleague while wearing them. Especially the Rhinos or Convicts!

Nevertheless, one of the many great things about living in London is that you can wear whatever you want without anyone really noticing, so if I want to wear JNCOs, I can.

So I wear JNCOs.

Admittedly, definitely not all the time and not everywhere, but they are becoming my default choice when I head out on my own.

They feel great to wear, with their heavy weight and excess of denim. I enjoy the look when I catch my own reflection in a window.

Calling them practical is probably an overstatement, though.

For sure, some people are looking at me and my stupid jeans, but not more than I can cope with. Somehow no strangers have commented on them yet, which is a big plus.

Buying advice

Finally, if you’re thinking about buying your first pair of JNCOs, I’d say go for it! You won’t regret it.

Which model to start with?

Twin Cannon is probably the most mainstream option, and it’s a good place to start. They are wide enough to be wider than what most people mean by baggy jeans, but they are still easy to wear and not as crazy as some other models.

But I think if I started from scratch now, I would probably pick the Jesters. They are the same fit as the Twin Cannon, but with nicer details.

What’s the waist size like?

Don’t get fooled by the wide legs. JNCO’s waist sizes are just like any other jeans. I’m normally a 32”, and it’s the same for JNCO.

What’s the length like?

I recommend sticking with your normal jeans length, not longer. I normally wear a 30” inseam for jeans, and that’s the length I wear for JNCO too. Even so, particularly for the wider models, the JNCOs drag on the ground a bit, and I step on the hems.

What’s the quality like?

Sadly, I never wore JNCOs in the 90s, so I can’t compare, but I’d say the modern ones are very good quality. I love how heavy the material is, and they are definitely well-made.
